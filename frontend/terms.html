Terms

WordPop is free to use. These terms cover the optional paid feature, WordPop Plus.

What WordPop Plus includes

The natural voices (Clara and Liam with a Canadian accent, Ava and Andrew with an American accent) that read your English entries and examples aloud on every device.

Free trial

Everyone can try the natural voices free for 7 days, starting the first time they listen with one. No card is needed, and nothing is charged when the trial ends – the app simply goes back to your phone's own voice.

Price and renewal

  • Monthly: CA$0.99 per month, or Yearly: CA$9.99 per year. Applicable taxes may be added.
  • Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel.
  • Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

Cancelling

Cancel any time in Settings → Voice → Manage subscription. You keep WordPop Plus until the end of the period you have paid for, and you won't be charged again. Deleting your account also cancels your subscription.

Refunds

Because there is a free trial, payments are generally non-refundable. If something went wrong – for example a double charge, or the voices not working for you – contact us within 14 days and we will make it right.

Changes

If the price changes, existing subscribers are told by email at least 30 days before their next renewal.

Contact

Questions about billing: reply to any WordPop email or write to the address shown on your Stripe receipt.
